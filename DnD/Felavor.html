Glasses of great teachings

Wondrous item, uncommon

Intrusive gaze.

As a bonus action you can learn a creature you can see intentions, if in combat, this will tell you one of three things
  • Who they plan to attack or target with a spell.
  • Whether they intend to use a non-standard reaction.
  • Where they plan on ending their next turn.
If used outside of combat, you can understand a creatures inner desires for the next 60 minutes. You cannot read its thoughts, but you can read its intentions.
