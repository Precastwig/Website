Godhood powers

Totenat has become a beloved household name, but we all wonder, what is the oracle text of his burgeoning godhood abilities? Here, questions like that, and only like that, are answered.

Improved Channel Divinity

You can use your channel divinity one extra time per rest

New Channel Divinity Abilities

Celestial Energy Harness

As a bonus action, you can touch your holy symbol and use Channel Divinity to regain one expended spell slot of your own, or two spells slots of an ally you can see. The spell slots regained must be level 3 or below.

Imbue Champion

When you roll initiative you may use Channel Divinity to declare any visible willing ally your champion.


For the remainder of the fight, you may cast spells as if you were in the location, with the sight and hearing of your champion. Spells cast this way get a -2 to saving throws and ranged spell attack rolls. Your champion can choose not to cast a spell this way.

Knife Robot

Wondrous item, legendary

This cute little robot has a little propeller hat and knives for hands. Very difficult to carry without accidentally poking yourself, Ouch!

Activate.

As a bonus action, the robot can be activated. Once activated, the little robot hovers above the activators head with its propeller. You may choose a mode for the robot to switch to at the start of each of your turns.

  • Attack:

    All attacks by the user are accompanied by the robot, the user gets advantage on all attack rolls and adds +1d10 to the damage roll.

  • Defence:

    All attacks on the user are partially blocked by the robot and have disadvantage, and all attack damage gets -1d10.
The robot lasts for 1 minute and becomes unusable garbage afterwards.

Yorrick

Wondrous item, legendary friend

This skull has well defined and gorgeous cheekbones. It has a skele-tonne of brilliant jokes that never get old, and has fantastic knowledge of all things. It’s really very excellent.

Innate magicks

Yorrick can ritual cast Detect Magic and Identify at will.

Local knowledge

Yorrick knows basic useful information about any area that it is in, it has a level of knowledge that a creature that lives here would have about the area.

Velsharoon’s Medallion

Wondrous item, legendary

This medallion is adorned with a thin gold chain, the medallion contains an embedded ruby, intricately carved into the shape of a bloodrose.

Veins of ice

You gain resistance to poison, acid and necrotic damage.

Velsharoon’s gift

Your wisdom is increased by 2, this may increase your wisdom above 20.

Sense of unease

Wearing this gives you an impending sense of doom, and the feeling that you’re being watched..
